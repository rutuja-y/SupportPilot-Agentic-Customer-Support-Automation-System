# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Standard Delivery

Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 business days after an order is shipped. Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays.

## Tracking an Order

Customers can ask support to check their order status. The system should use the order database for the latest status rather than relying on this document.

## Delayed Orders

If an order is marked as delayed, the customer should contact support for assistance. The support system may create a ticket for further investigation.

## Delivery Dates

Estimated delivery dates are estimates and are not guaranteed. The support assistant must not promise an exact delivery date unless it is confirmed by an authorized source.

## Missing Delivery Information

If the available order record does not contain a delivery date, the assistant must explain that the date is unavailable rather than inventing one.
