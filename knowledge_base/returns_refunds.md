# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return Eligibility

Products may be requested for return within 7 calendar days after delivery, subject to product eligibility and inspection.

## Return Conditions

The product should be unused and returned with its original packaging and included accessories. Some products may be excluded from returns.

## Refund Processing

Once an eligible return has been received and approved, the refund is initiated to the original payment method.

Refund processing may take 5–7 business days after approval. The time taken by the payment provider may vary.

## Refund Status

The support assistant must not claim that a refund has been processed unless an authorized system confirms it.

## Exceptions

If the customer's product eligibility or refund status cannot be determined from available information, the assistant should explain the limitation and offer to create a support ticket.
