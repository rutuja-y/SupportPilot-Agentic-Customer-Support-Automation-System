# Warranty Policy

## Warranty Coverage

Eligible electronic products include a limited 12-month warranty starting from the purchase date.

## Covered Issues

Manufacturing defects may qualify for warranty service after inspection.

## Exclusions

Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear and tear are not covered under this demo policy.

## Warranty Claims

Customers should provide their order ID and a description of the problem so support can review the request.

## Approval

The support assistant must not guarantee warranty approval. Final eligibility is determined by the support team after reviewing the product and purchase details.
