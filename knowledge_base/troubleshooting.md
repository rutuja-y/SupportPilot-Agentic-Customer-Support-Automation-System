# Basic Product Troubleshooting Guide

## Wireless Headphones

If the headphones do not connect:

1. Check that they are charged.
2. Turn Bluetooth off and on again on the device.
3. Remove the headphones from the device's paired-device list.
4. Restart the headphones and try pairing again.

## Wireless Mouse

If the mouse does not respond:

1. Check the battery or charging level.
2. Ensure the wireless receiver is connected, if applicable.
3. Try a different USB port.
4. Restart the computer and reconnect the mouse.

## Mechanical Keyboard

If some keys do not respond:

1. Disconnect and reconnect the keyboard.
2. Try another USB port.
3. Check whether the issue occurs on another computer.

## When Troubleshooting Fails

If the problem continues after these steps, support should offer to create a ticket for further investigation.

## Safety

Customers should not disassemble electronic products or attempt electrical repairs as part of basic troubleshooting.
